# The Organisational Guide

The half of the work that decides whether any of it survives.

Every source this handbook draws on — the WHO's own analyses, the peer-reviewed literature, and ten
years of AUCOOP's records across four countries — says the same thing: these projects fail for
organisational and financial reasons, not technical ones. The WHO puts the non-technical share of
what determines success at roughly **80%**.

And yet every guide, including the first versions of this one, gives that 80% a few paragraphs and
the servers twenty pages. This part corrects that. It is written as procedure — steps, what you
bring, what you produce, what goes wrong — because *"engage stakeholders"* is advice and *"here are
the six people, the two questions, and the order"* is a recipe.

Do these in order. Each one produces something the next one needs.

| | Recipe | Produces |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **[Securing the mandate](Mandate.md)** | A director who has said yes to staff, in person, and a signed agreement |
| 2 | **[Stakeholder conversations](Stakeholders.md)** | Six answered interviews and a list of what actually takes too long |
| 3 | **[Choosing and setting up super-users](Super-Users.md)** | Two named people with time allowance and accountability |
| 4 | **[Retiring paper](Retiring-Paper.md)** | One process that runs digital-only from a fixed date |
| 5 | **[Building the support ladder](Support-Ladder.md)** | Three tiers with a name and route at each |
| 6 | **[Funding the owner](Funding-The-Owner.md)** | The case for an IT post, with a number, put to the board |
| 7 | **[Training by role](Training.md)** | Seven different sessions for seven different jobs |
| 8 | **[Writing the handover](Handover.md)** | The pack that stays, and the page about what you did not do |

!!! warning "From the archive — why this part exists"

    In 2017 a UPC thesis deploying Bahmni in Ethiopia identified, before deployment, that the
    decisive conditions were *"independent of the medical record system"*: reliable power, training
    that remains accessible over time, and a technician on site. It then wrote a continuity chapter
    in which the only actors were the visiting students, and no local person was named, funded or
    made accountable. The project was not built on. That is the whole argument of this part, made
    nine years ago and then lost.
