# Training by role

!!! warning "Outline — not yet written"

Different people need completely different things, and a single all-staff session serves none of them
well.

| Role | Needs | Session |
|---|---|---|
| **Registration clerk** | One workflow, done fast and correctly. Finding an existing patient. | Short, repeated, at the desk |
| **Nurse** | Recording observations on a ward round; what to do when the system is down | On the ward, in a real shift |
| **Pharmacist** | Dispensing and stock movement, in the two screens that matter | At the counter |
| **Laboratory technician** | Receiving an order, entering a result, releasing it | In the lab |
| **Medical officer** | Finding a history, ordering, prescribing; the reports they are entitled to | Short, and their time is expensive |
| **Administrator / matron** | The monthly return, produced unaided | Whole session, before month end |
| **IT officer / super-user** | Everything above, plus backup, restart, user accounts, escalation | Days, not hours |
| **Director / board** | What it does, what it costs, what happens if nobody maintains it | One meeting, no software |

**The chapter must open by conceding the point**: training was delivered at Lunsar in 2024, to
technical and management staff, documented and certified by a partner institution — and adoption did
not follow. Training is necessary and it is not sufficient. What is missing is
[accountability, a support route, and something that depends on the system](../2-Story/2.11-Nobody-Used-It.md).

**Also to cover:** the super-user model and how to choose them; training the trainer; and materials
that survive without you — short task cards, in-product help, screen recordings.
