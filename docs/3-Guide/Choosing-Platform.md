4. When neither is right, and something simpler is.

**An honest note that belongs on this page:** Bahmni and the wider OpenMRS family have a documented
multi-country, multi-year record of deployments. GNU Health's published longitudinal evidence is much
thinner. That does not make GNU Health the wrong choice — its single-database architecture and small
footprint are real advantages in a constrained setting — but the case for it should be made on those
grounds rather than on track record.
