# St. Jean de Dieu, Yassa–Douala, Cameroon

!!! warning "Outline — not yet written"

## Why this case matters

It is the contrast. Similar constraints — unreliable power, limited connectivity, paper records,
varying digital literacy — and a different outcome.

The difference most visible in the records: the hospital's administrative board had already
prioritised digitalisation before the team arrived. Rooms were made available, staff time was
released, and the project had institutional backing. There was also a pair of local champions — the
hospital's IT technician and the director.

**Douala is the site that adopted.**

## The honest caveats

- The comparison spans **one year**, not eight — Douala is a first edition.
- The board was not the only difference; the super-user pair may matter as much or more.
- Whether Lunsar's more elaborate network helped, hindered, or made no difference is an open
  question that has been put directly to someone who worked at both sites. That answer belongs here
  when it arrives.
