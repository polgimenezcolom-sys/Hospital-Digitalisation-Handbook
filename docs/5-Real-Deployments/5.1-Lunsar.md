# St. John of God Hospital, Lunsar, Sierra Leone

!!! warning "Outline — not yet written"

## Context

A mission hospital in Port Loko District. Roughly 100–150 outpatient visits a day and 20–25
inpatients at any time. Staff: 6 doctors, 25 nurses, 3 laboratory technicians, 2 pharmacists,
2 clerks.

**Bed capacity is recorded three different ways** across project documents — 25, 85 and 100. The
hospital's own returns to the Ministry and to the church health association both give 85; 20–25 is
the daily census. This unresolved figure is itself the clearest illustration of why
[D-1](../4-Instruments/D1-Data-Capture.md) exists, and it should be reported honestly rather than
quietly resolved.

## What to cover

**The trajectory, 2018–2026.** Requirements elicited from the administrator in 2018 — stock control,
retrievable clinical history, compatibility with the existing accounting system. A platform shortlist
drawn up the same year that a different team arrived at again eight years later. Then: an academic
management system (2018), a form-collection deployment (2023), a campus network (2024–25), and a full
hospital information system (2026).

**Infrastructure as found and as built.** Satellite uplink. A mesh of around twenty-eight
inexpensive access points on OpenWrt. A fibre road crossing chosen because glass carries no
conductor and cannot carry a lightning strike between buildings. Unresolved: whether the cable to
Emergency was ever run.

**What happened after.** In July 2026, four months after the hospital information system was
deployed, every statutory monthly return was still being produced by hand.

**What we would do differently.** This is the section that matters, and it should be blunt: the level
deployed was above what the institution could carry, and there was no instrument at the time that
made that visible before the decision.
