# CMAM paediatric centre, Meki, Ethiopia

**2017 · Bahmni · Current state: unknown**

## What is known

A paediatric centre and orphanage in Meki, Oromia, run within the Community Management of Acute
Malnutrition programme of the NGO Alegría Sin Fronteras. Around a hundred patients a day. Three
usable computers, unreliable electricity, staff with — the thesis's words — *"poca habilitat
ofimàtica"*. Registration was double: paper and a spreadsheet, with several unsynchronised versions.

The 2017 UPC bachelor's thesis (Bonet, supervised by Eva Vidal — [hdl 2117/106696](https://hdl.handle.net/2117/106696))
compared thirteen open-source systems and tested three, chose Bahmni, and prepared a pilot for
August 2017: Bahmni 0.88 on CentOS on a donated server behind two 650 VA UPS units; registration and
consultation first, lab and pharmacy installed but held back *"until administrators, health workers
and doctors are used to the registration and visit system"*; Bahmni Connect on tablets for outages;
a RACHEL education server on a Raspberry Pi for the orphanage; two weeks of on-site training; a
staff questionnaire and WHO monitoring questions for evaluation; a contingency kit including a
**tested clone of the server disk**.

Its own statement of purpose: *"to set a methodology in order to implement a medical record system"*,
*"with the intention of applying it to different health centres"*. Its own pre-mortem: the decisive
conditions are *"independent of the medical record system"* — power, accessible training, a
technician on site — and without them the system *"can weaken the proper functioning of the health
centre"*.

Material cost 691 €; travel 1,700 €.

## What is not known

Whether the August 2017 pilot went as planned. Whether the evaluation questionnaire was ever
administered. Whether the system ran past the volunteers' departure. Anything at all after May 2017.

## Why it matters

This is the handbook, nine years early — the same platform, the same requirements, the same phasing,
the same diagnosis of what actually decides success. Its continuity chapter names no local owner and
no recurring budget; every sentence's subject is *"we"*. *"This project is a pilot and continuity is
inherently implied."* It was not built on, and the 2026 team did not know it existed.

Its best contents are reusable now: the registration-simplification recipe, the staff evaluation
questionnaire, the contingency kit, and the itemised budget format. See the
[technical guide](../3B-Technical-Guide/index.md) and [resources](../resources.md).
