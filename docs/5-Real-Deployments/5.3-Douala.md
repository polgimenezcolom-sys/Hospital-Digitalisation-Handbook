# St. Jean de Dieu, Yassa–Douala, Cameroon

**2026 · Current state: adopted**

## Why this case matters

It is the contrast. Similar constraints — unreliable power, limited connectivity, paper records,
varying digital literacy — and a different outcome.

## What is known

The hospital's administrative board had prioritised digitalisation **before** the team arrived. Rooms
were made available for training, staff time was released, and the project had visible institutional
backing. There was a pair of local champions: the hospital's IT technician and the director.

The network was deliberately modest: around **2,000 €** of access points, no VLANs, aerial cabling
done by the hospital's own technician, plus roughly 2,000–2,500 € of tablets. Considerably less
elaborate than Lunsar's mesh — at the site that adopted.

The Cameroon questionnaire (31 questions) was answered in June 2026 by the one volunteer who has
worked at both sites; a comparative Lunsar-vs-Douala instrument was sent in July 2026 and is pending.

## The honest caveats

- The comparison spans **one year**, not ten — Douala is a first edition.
- The board was not the only difference; the super-user pair may matter as much.
- Whether Lunsar's more elaborate network helped, hindered, or made no difference has been asked
  directly of the person who worked at both. That answer belongs here when it arrives.
- Service volumes (beds, staff, consultations per day) are not yet recorded — Douala is described
  qualitatively where Lunsar has a table.
