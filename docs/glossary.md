# Glossary

Terms and acronyms used in this handbook. Expanded here once, rather than on every page.

## Health and organisational

**ANC** — Antenatal care. Care given during pregnancy before delivery.

**Bed state** — A daily or monthly count of occupied and available beds, per ward. One of the
statutory returns.

**CHASL** — Christian Health Association of Sierra Leone. The church health association mission
hospitals report to, in addition to the Ministry.

**EMR / EHR** — Electronic Medical Record / Electronic Health Record. The clinical core: a patient's
history across encounters.

**Folder ID** — The number a hospital assigns to a patient at first registration, written on a card
and on the paper folder. In a hospital without national identity documents, this is the join key of
the entire record system.

**HIS** — Hospital Information System. A system covering clinical records *and* the administrative
side: pharmacy stock, laboratory, billing, reporting.

**IPD / OPD** — Inpatient Department / Outpatient Department.

**MoHS** — Ministry of Health and Sanitation (Sierra Leone).

**Morbidity return** — A monthly count of cases by diagnosis. Statutory.

**MoU** — Memorandum of Understanding. The written agreement between the deploying organisation and
the hospital.

**Super-user** — A member of hospital staff trained beyond ordinary use, who becomes the first line
of support on site.

## Technical

**AVR** — Automatic Voltage Regulation. A UPS feature that corrects voltage sag and surge rather than
passing it through. Essential where grid voltage swings between 170 V and 260 V.

**Bahmni** — An open-source hospital information system combining OpenMRS for clinical records with
separate systems for pharmacy, laboratory and reporting.

**CGNAT** — Carrier-Grade Network Address Translation. Where an internet provider shares one public
address between many customers. Outbound connections work; nothing can connect inwards, which breaks
remote support unless a VPN is used.

**DHIS2** — District Health Information Software 2. The aggregate health reporting platform used
nationally in many countries, including Sierra Leone.

**GNU Health** — An open-source hospital information system built on the Tryton framework, using a
single database for clinical and administrative data.

**KoBoToolbox / ODK** — Form-based data collection platforms. Level 1 on the tool spectrum.

**OpenMRS** — The most widely deployed open-source clinical record system in low-resource settings.
Deliberately clinical only: no billing or stock management.

**PoE** — Power over Ethernet. Powering a device, typically an access point, over its network cable.

**UPS** — Uninterruptible Power Supply. Battery backup, usually combined with voltage regulation.

**VPN / WireGuard** — An encrypted tunnel between machines. WireGuard is a modern implementation,
small enough to run on low-powered hardware and able to reconnect silently when addresses change.

**VPS** — Virtual Private Server. A rented virtual machine with a public address, used here as the
meeting point for a VPN when the hospital is behind CGNAT.
