# B-1 · Readiness assessment

**When:** before you travel, revised on site.
**Produces:** a score that feeds [B-2](B2-Level-Decision.md).

## What it does

Scores the gap between what the proposed system *assumes* and what the hospital *is*. Built on the
design–reality gap model: rate each dimension 0–10 for the distance between assumption and reality.
Higher total, higher risk of failure.

| Dimension | What you are rating | Score |
|---|---|---|
| **Information** | What the system expects recorded vs. what is written down today | 0–10 |
| **Technology** | Power, network, hardware assumed vs. installed and working | 0–10 |
| **Processes** | The workflow the software implies vs. the journey in [D-3](D3-Workflow-Mapping.md) | 0–10 |
| **Objectives and values** | What the deploying team wants vs. what the hospital wants | 0–10 |
| **Staffing and skills** | Digital literacy assumed vs. present on the ward | 0–10 |
| **Management systems** | Governance and accountability assumed vs. actual | 0–10 |
| **Other resources** | Money and time assumed vs. committed | 0–10 |

## The four questions the model does not ask

These decide survival rather than fit, and they are yes/no:

| Code | Question |
|---|---|
| `PAID-IT` | Is there a paid IT person at the hospital? |
| `OWNER-5Y` | Is there a funded owner for the next five years? |
| `PRESENCE` | How long is the organisation committed to this site? (years) |
| `HANDOVER` | Will a handover pack ([L-1](L1-Handover-Pack.md)) exist on departure? |

!!! danger "Open — not yet settled"

    The thresholds that map a total score to a level in B-2 are **not fixed**. They should be
    calibrated by scoring Lunsar and Yassa–Douala retrospectively, where the outcomes are known, and
    deriving the boundaries from that.

    Until then, use the score as a structured conversation rather than as a rule.
