## The six people

Find each by name and speak to them separately. A group meeting produces agreement and no commitment.

| Role | Why they decide whether this works |
|---|---|
| **Medical Director** | The only person who can make this an expectation rather than a suggestion — and must say so to staff directly |
| **Head of Nursing** | Nurses do the largest volume of data entry in any hospital |
| **Head of Pharmacy** | Owns stock; first to notice if dispensing gets slower |
| **Laboratory Director** | Owns the result chain, where early wins are most visible |
| **Administrator / Finance** | Owns billing and the monthly returns. Ask what finance system already exists |
| **IT Officer** | Becomes the super-user and the owner. If the post does not exist, see the stop rule |

Ask each of them: **what takes too long in your unit right now?** and **what would have to be true
for this to be worth your staff's time?**

## The agreement must cover

- Who owns the system — the hospital. Say it explicitly.
- Who owns the data, and how patient consent is handled
- Training commitments: how many staff, released for how long, when
- Remote support: what the organisation will and will not do after leaving, and for how long
- **Who pays for what after you go** — hosting, hardware replacement, the IT post

That last line predicts survival and is the one most often left out.
