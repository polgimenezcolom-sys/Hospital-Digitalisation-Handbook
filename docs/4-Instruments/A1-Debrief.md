# A-1 · Debrief and feedback

**When:** started in the field, closed within two weeks.
**Produces:** what goes back into this handbook.

## Design constraints — not preferences

- **Part of the closure report you already have to write.** A new voluntary task will not be
  completed; an extension of a mandatory one will.
- **Completed in the field, before you fly.** People land and stop.
- **Under two hours in total.** A half-filled form is worse than none, because it looks like data.
- **One named person merges returns** into the next version, on a fixed cadence.

## What it asks

- [ ] Which handbook version did you follow?
- [ ] Which instruments did you complete, and which did you skip? Why?
      *A skipped instrument is a finding about the handbook, not a failure of the team.*
- [ ] Where did the handbook say something that turned out to be wrong on site?
- [ ] What did you have to invent because it was not covered?
- [ ] Scores against the outcomes in [D-4](D4-Exit-Gates.md), at departure
- [ ] The three questions you wish you had asked while you were still there

!!! danger "Open — not yet settled"

    Two things are unresolved and the loop does not work without them:

    1. **Can this genuinely be attached to the existing closure report?** Is that format fixed by the
       funder, or can it be extended? If it cannot, the feedback loop needs a different host.
    2. **Who owns the merge, and how often?** Without a named owner and a cadence, three versions of
       this handbook will exist within two years and none will be canonical.

Then write your deployment up properly: [case study template](../contributing/case-study.md).
